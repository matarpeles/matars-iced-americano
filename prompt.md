# Our Company

**Mid-size F&B tech company** — 200 engineers, 40 microservices, Series C, expanding globally.

Engineers spend more time **finding owners** than fixing issues.

**Agents, skills, and MCPs** are sprawling with no visibility or governance.

# Our Goals

**As an on-call engineer**, I need service owners and runbooks visible instantly during incidents.

**As a developer**, I need all services and agentic assets in one place for fast onboarding.

# IDP Setting

Microservices on **EKS** with **GitHub**, **PagerDuty**, and **Datadog**. 

3 environments (dev, staging, prod). Mono-repo structure. **ArgoCD** for deployments. **Terraform** for infrastructure.
